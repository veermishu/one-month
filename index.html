a scrapbook, sort of — June 3 to July 27

Not Mystery.
Mishu.

It started on Instagram — just talking, the way talking quietly turns into something before you notice it has. On June 3rd we finally met, Starbucks, both of us more nervous than we let on. Somewhere between the awkward small talk and the not-so-awkward silences, I knew I liked you. On June 27th I asked you, unofficially. Three days later I worked up the nerve to make it official.

This is everything since.

one month, scroll on

June 19

Hav Coffee

Day one, sort of

Our first real date. The café was quiet, we ordered coffees neither of us could finish — you didn't like yours, so you took mine without asking. I didn't mind. I think that's when I knew.

June 30

The FRIENDS Event

Officially, this time

I asked you to be mine — for real this time. No more "unofficially." You said yes, and it's been my favorite word since.

July 8

Timezone

Arcade & bowling

You beat me at air hockey and basketball — fair and square, you're just better. I got a trophy though, so I'm calling it a win. Before you left, you gave me the "okay jaanu" kiss. Movie reference, but it's one of my favorite memories anyway.

July 17

A Rooftop, Uninvited

Lunch, then a first

Lunch date, then we snuck into a random building and found our way to the roof, no permission asked. That's where I finally got the guts to ask for a kiss. You said yes. It was your first. I hope I did it justice.

July 22

Main Vapas Aaunga

Movie night

I keep teasing you that even if you ever left, main vapas aaunga. Half a joke, whole truth. I mean it every time I say it.

July 25

Borivali, aka "Gaav"

Maggi & a pillow fight

You came over to what you insist on calling my "gaav." We made Maggi, had a full-blown pillow fight, and nearly got caught by your parents. My heart has never raced that fast over instant noodles.

July 27

One Month

Unofficially, officially, however you count it

It's been a month. I like you way more than I probably should admit in writing. Can't believe it's already been this long — and also that it's only been this long.

here's to month two,

and every one after it.
